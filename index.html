01 / The story

Some ideas take
a different shape.

UNO's first chapter was meant for the real world. Its next chapter found a home on the internet.

CHAPTER 01 / THE ORIGINAL IDEA

Made for a different world.

UNO began as the name and visual identity for a water-based paint business. There was a concept, a logo, and an ambition to take the idea from testing into production.

CHAPTER 02 / THE PAUSE

The paint stayed a plan.

Funding ran out before the project could reach production. The paint never made it to store shelves. The project paused, but the identity remained.

CHAPTER 03 / THE TURN

A new kind of canvas.

Later, UNO's creator brought the name and logo into a different world: Solana. A brand concept once intended for paint cans became a token with a new story to tell.

CHAPTER 04 / WHAT COMES NEXT

The next coat is unwritten.

UNO's internet chapter is still being written. Art, ideas, and community can shape what comes next. No pretend legacy. No finished script.

02 / The manifesto

Not every unfinished thing is the end of the story.

UNO was never a billion-dollar paint empire. It was an idea that didn't get the resources to become a real product. That's the truth, and there's no reason to dress it up.

The name survived. The logo survived. Then the idea found an unexpected second life online.

This isn't a claim that the old business succeeded. It's a new chapter built from something that already existed. The canvas changed. The name didn't.

06 / Safety & FAQ

Read this
before you buy.

New tokens attract scammers. A few minutes here can save you a lot of trouble.

Golden rules

  • Never share your recovery phrase. Nobody from UNO will ever ask for it.
  • Nobody here will DM you first. Treat every unsolicited message as a scam.
  • Copy the contract address from this page, then compare it on Solscan.
  • Only spend what you can afford to lose.
How do I verify the contract address?

Copy the address from this page, then open it on Solscan or pump.fun and check that every character matches. The same address is listed on Coinvote. If one character is different, it is a different token.

How do I spot a fake UNO?

Anyone can create a token with the same name or ticker. Only the contract address matters. Ignore look-alike tokens, links from replies or DMs, and "airdrop" pages that ask you to connect your wallet and sign something you don't understand.

Why does the address end with “pump”?

Tokens launched on pump.fun get addresses that end in “pump”. It's a normal marker of the launch platform, not a sign of trust or risk by itself.

What can I expect from UNO?

UNO is a story still being written, and we're giving it our best. With the community's support, our only aim is success. That said, no one can promise how it turns out. Crypto assets are volatile, so only put in what you're comfortable losing. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

What is the real story behind UNO?

UNO began as the name and identity of a water-based paint business. It was more than a name on a logo: sample batches of the paint were actually made. But the project never reached mass production, and the paint never made it to store shelves. Its creator later brought the name and logo to Solana.

Where can I ask for help?

In the Telegram group. Ask there, but never post your recovery phrase or send funds to anyone who contacts you privately.
